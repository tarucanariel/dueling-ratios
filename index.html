How to Play

Objective

AAT's Dueling Ratios helps you practice fraction (and, optionally, integer) arithmetic — addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division — by working through each problem step by step, the same way you would on paper.

Game Modes

  • Solo — practice on your own, at your own pace.
  • Same Device — two players take turns on one screen.
  • vs Computer — take turns against a computer opponent on one screen. Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard — higher difficulties answer faster and make fewer mistakes.
  • Online — challenge someone on a different device using a shareable room code.

How to Solve a Problem

Each problem is broken into a series of steps, shown as empty boxes. One box glows at a time — that's the one asking for an answer. Tap the matching number from the pool to fill it in. A correct answer earns a point and moves you to the next step; a wrong answer costs a point, but the same box stays open to try again.

The steps look a little different depending on the operation:

  • Similar fractions (same denominator) — add or subtract the numerators directly.
  • Dissimilar fractions (different denominators) — first find the Lowest Common Denominator (LCD), convert each fraction, then combine them.
  • Multiplication — multiply straight across: numerators together, denominators together.
  • Division — flip the second fraction, then multiply.

If the result can be simplified, you'll get one more step to reduce it — and if it simplifies all the way to a whole number, you'll enter just that one number instead of a fraction.

Scoring

Every correct answer is worth +1 point; every wrong guess costs -1 point. Whichever player has the higher score once all the fraction pairs are solved wins the game.

Streaks

Answer correctly several times in a row within a single game to build a streak. Every 5 in a row (5, 10, 15, 20, and onward) pops up a celebration banner with its own sound — the color and chime escalate through four tiers as your streak climbs, then loop back around for even longer runs. A single wrong answer resets the streak back to zero.

Setup Options

  • Operations — choose which operations to practice, or select all of them.
  • Allow negatives — turns on negative numbers, adding integer sign rules to the practice.
  • Number of fractions — how many problems to solve in one game (1–5).
  • Time control — an optional shared clock. Each player's time only counts down on their own turn, and running out of time ends the game immediately.

Tiebreakers

If both players finish with the same score, whoever has more time remaining wins the tiebreaker — but only if a timer was turned on. Without a timer, a tied score stays a tie.

Playing Online

One player creates a game and receives a short room code; the other player enters that code to join. Once both players are in, the game begins automatically for both of you at the same time.

Signing In with Google (optional)

Typing a name is always enough to play — signing in with Google is entirely optional, and only unlocks two extra things: your stats following your account across every device instead of just one browser, and the ability to join a class. Look for the "Sign in with Google" option next to the name field on the setup screen.

My Stats

Available once you're signed in with Google. Shows how many games you've played and your accuracy, broken out separately for Solo, Same Device, vs Computer, and Online, plus a combined "All Modes" total — and, in its own "My Badges" section, every achievement badge you've earned so far. Tap a badge (or hover it on a computer) to see its name and description.

Achievement Badges

Signed-in players can permanently unlock badges by hitting certain milestones. Earning one pops up its own gold celebration banner the moment it happens, in addition to showing up in My Stats afterward. Current badges:

  • 🌱 Getting Started — Play 5 games.
  • 📚 Regular Player — Play 25 games.
  • 🏆 Dedicated — Play 100 games.
  • 🎯 Perfect Game — Finish a game with zero misses.
  • 💯 Sharpshooter — 90%+ accuracy over at least 50 total answers.
  • 🔥 On a Roll — Reach a 10-streak in a single game.
  • ⚡ Unstoppable — Reach a 20-streak in a single game.
  • 🌟 Flawless Fifty — Reach a 50-streak in a single game.
  • ⏱️ Beat the Clock — Finish a game (Local or Online) with a time control turned on.
  • 🧮 Math Machine — Answer 1,000 questions correctly, lifetime.
  • 🎓 Math Whiz — 98%+ accuracy over at least 200 total answers.

My Class

Also inside My Stats, once signed in. A teacher (signed in with a school account) can create up to 5 classes, each with its own short join code to share — students who join with that code show up in a roster showing each student's games played and accuracy. A teacher can rename a class, remove an individual student from the roster, or delete a class entirely once it's no longer needed, freeing up a slot for a new one. A student can only be in one class at a time; joining a new one replaces the last. If a student's class is ever deleted, or the teacher removes them from it, that's picked up automatically the next time they open My Class.

Watch Games

Lets a teacher (signed in with a school account) browse and spectate ongoing Online games in real time, without taking part.

Worksheets

Generates a printable practice sheet — the same kinds of problems as the game, laid out with blank boxes to fill in by hand, plus an answer key on the last page. Choose the operations, negatives, and how many problems (1–10), then print or save as a PDF straight from your browser's print dialog. No sign-in needed.

Send Feedback

Once signed in with Google, look for the 💬 button in the bottom-right corner of the setup screen. Use it to send a comment, bug report, or suggestion straight to the developer — it only appears on the setup screen itself, not mid-game.
